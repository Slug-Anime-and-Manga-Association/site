About SlugCon

SlugCon is UCSC's annual student-run anime convention, brought to you by the Slug Anime and Manga Association! UCSC's nerd culture is showcased through panels, performances, a maid cafe, an artist showcase, and more; all made possible with the hard work of our students and student organizations. SlugCon is open to all students to attend and participate in, free of charge!

SlugCon 2026 will be our 9th SlugCon since its inception in 2017. SAMA is thrilled to be able to bring this event back to UCSC once again!

About SAMA

The Slug Anime and Manga Association (or SAMA) is UCSC's anime club! We promote the understanding of Japanese popular culture through the medium of anime, manga, games, and other forms of Japanese media. With over 2000 members, SAMA strives to create a community where nerd culture can thrive!

Matsuri Mari & Merch

Inspired by Snow and Sakura Mikus, our seasonal Mari (SAMA mascot) design contest returned again for SlugCon 2026! This year, we held a contest for a Matsuri Mari to be the face of SlugCon 2026! The winner, who was voted for in-person at our Winter Art Contest, received all merch featuring their design, guaranteed tabling at SlugCon 2026, and a chance to take a life-sized cutout home!

Our winner was Brooke!!

Here's a collection of all of the other designs submitted this year!

We also held a SAMA 2025-2026 t-shirt and hoodie design contest for our fundraising efforts! Winners received a merch bundle, including their design!

The winning designs are available for purchase at SlugCon! All shirts are $30 each, with hoodies being $50 each.

Thank you all for participating.
